# Teacher Student Tracking - Quick Start Guide

## Dashboard Overview

### Accessing Student Analytics
1. Log in as a teacher
2. Navigate to **Teacher Dashboard** (`/teacher/dashboard`)
3. You'll see 4 key metrics:
   - **Courses Created** - Total courses you've published
   - **Problems Created** - Coding challenges you've designed
   - **Active Students** - Unique students across all your courses
   - **Total Enrollments** - Sum of all course enrollments

### View All Students
Click the **"Student Analytics"** card or visit `/teacher/students`

**Features:**
- 🔍 **Search**: Find students by name or email
- 📊 **Sort**: By XP points, name, or courses enrolled
- 👤 **Student Cards**: Show XP, level, and enrolled courses
- 🔗 **Quick Access**: Click any student to view detailed performance

### View Individual Student Performance
Click on any student card to see:

**Student Profile:**
- Level and XP points
- Daily streak count

**Per-Course Breakdown:**
- **Lessons**: Progress bar showing completed/total lessons
- **Quizzes**: List of recent attempts with scores and percentages
- **Problems**: Count of solved problems

**Performance Indicators:**
- 🟢 Green = 70%+ (Good)
- 🟡 Yellow = 50-69% (OK)
- 🔴 Red = Below 50% (Needs Attention)

### View Course Analytics
From **Manage Courses** (`/teacher/courses`):
1. Click **"View Analytics"** on any course card
2. Navigate through 5 tabs:

#### **Overview Tab**
- Total students, lessons, quizzes, problems
- Recent enrollments and activity
- Average completion rates
- Top 5 performing students

#### **Students Tab**
- Full student roster table
- Progress percentages for each student
- Lessons, quizzes, and problems completed
- Average quiz scores
- "View Details" button for each student

#### **Lessons Tab**
- Completion rate for each lesson
- Visual progress bars
- Number of students who completed each lesson

#### **Quizzes Tab**
- Average scores and percentages per quiz
- Total attempts and unique students
- Difficulty level indicators

#### **Problems Tab**
- Number of students who solved each problem
- Total attempts per problem
- Success rate percentage

## Common Use Cases

### Identify Struggling Students
1. Go to **Course Analytics**
2. Click **Students** tab
3. Look for progress percentages below 50%
4. Click "View Details" to see specific areas of difficulty

### Track Course Effectiveness
1. Open **Course Analytics**
2. Check **Lessons** tab for completion rates
3. Review **Quizzes** tab for average scores
4. Low completion/scores indicate content may need revision

### Monitor Engagement
1. Check **Overview** tab in Course Analytics
2. Look at "Recent Activity" (quiz attempts in last 30 days)
3. Compare "Recent Enrollments" vs "Total Students"

### Find Top Performers
1. Go to **Student Analytics** (`/teacher/students`)
2. Sort by "XP Points (High to Low)"
3. Or check **Overview** tab in Course Analytics for top 5

## Tips for Teachers

### Best Practices
✅ Review student progress weekly
✅ Identify patterns in quiz performance
✅ Reach out to students with low completion rates
✅ Celebrate top performers
✅ Use analytics to improve course content

### Red Flags to Watch For
⚠️ Student enrolled but 0% progress
⚠️ Quiz scores consistently below 50%
⚠️ Multiple attempts on same problem without solving
⚠️ Sudden drop in activity after initial engagement

### Navigation Shortcuts
- **Dashboard**: `/teacher/dashboard`
- **All Students**: `/teacher/students`
- **Manage Courses**: `/teacher/courses`
- **Course Analytics**: Click "View Analytics" from course card
- **Student Details**: Click student card from Students page
